\# Parakh (परख): Lifestyle Disease Screening Parakh is a free bilingual (English \& Hindi) tool to help estimate your risk of developing 4 lifestyle diseases:



1\. Diabetes (Type 2)



2\. Heart Disease



3\. Chronic Kidney Disease



4\. Fatty Liver



The tool has been developed for rural India with community health workers in mind. However, anyone can use it. It is completely browser based so no servers, no accounts, and no personal data ever gets sent to a server.



\## How does it work



There are two ways to screen:



Quick Mode: This uses factors from your everyday life such as age, weight, family history etc. No lab results needed.



Clinical Mode: A much more accurate result using real lab results such as blood sugar, cholesterol, kidney function etc.



We do not estimate any calculations. Our clinical mode uses real machine learning models trained on 36,809 rows of real medical data from the CDC's NHANES public health survey. You can find out exactly how accurate our models are by clicking on Inside the models section of our website.



\## Can I use this instead of seeing a doctor?



No. Parakh is a screening tool and not a diagnostic tool. Think of it as a smoke alarm. It will tell you if there may be a fire (health risk) but won't stop the fire. If you recieve a result of High Risk please see a doctor/health worker.



\## Privacy



Your health data is very sensitive. This tool will never send your numbers to a server. Everything is done offline on your device.



\## How to use the website



No installation required. Simply download this folder and open index.html. It will open in your web browser.



\## Developers



If you want to check the models or re-train them:



Training code can be found in scripts/.



Model weights and accuracy can be found in models.js and data/models.json.



The raw data is not included in this repo (too large). However you can download it yourself by running the scripts.
